Zorvex Robotics Employee Handbook

Welcome to Zorvex Robotics. Zorvex Robotics was founded in 2014 in Pune by Meera Kulkarni and Daniel Osei. The company designs warehouse sorting robots, and its best known product is the Kestrel K7 sorting arm. The head office is on the fourth floor of the Lumen Tech Park in Hinjewadi. Zorvex currently employs around 340 people across offices in Pune, Hyderabad and Rotterdam. This handbook explains the policies that apply to all full-time employees.

Working hours and remote work. Core working hours are from 10:30 AM to 4:00 PM India time, and every employee should be reachable during core hours. Outside core hours, employees may plan their own schedule as long as they work 40 hours per week. Employees may work remotely for up to two days per week. Remote days must be logged in the HelmDesk portal by Friday of the previous week. Employees in their first 60 days must work from the office every day. Team meetings are held every Tuesday at 11:00 AM, and attendance in person is expected.

Leave policy. Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 8 unused days can be carried over into the next year, and any days above that are lost on 31 March. Employees also receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for any sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days. Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver. Leave requests of more than 5 working days must be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance.

Equipment and expenses. Every new employee receives a laptop on their first day; engineers receive a 16-inch laptop and all other staff receive a 14-inch laptop. Employees may claim a one-time home office allowance of 15,000 rupees in their first year. The monthly internet allowance is 1,200 rupees. Expense claims must be submitted within 45 days of the purchase, with a receipt, through the HelmDesk portal. Claims are reimbursed in the next salary payment after approval. Any single purchase above 25,000 rupees needs written approval from a department head before it is made.

Learning and development. Each employee has an annual learning budget of 40,000 rupees, which can be used for courses, certifications and conference tickets. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next year. Employees who complete an approved certification receive an extra day of paid leave. Every quarter, the company runs an internal hackathon called Forge Week, and the winning team receives a trophy and a team dinner.

Performance reviews. Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from two peers chosen by the employee, and a meeting with the manager. Ratings use a five-point scale where 3 means meets expectations. Salary revisions take effect on 1 July each year and are based on the April review.

Security and IT. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, HelmDesk and the code repository. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours, by calling extension 4411. Visitors must be signed in at reception and wear a red visitor badge at all times. Personal USB drives are not allowed on office computers.

Health and wellbeing. All full-time employees are covered by group health insurance with a sum insured of 5 lakh rupees, which also covers a spouse and up to two children. The office gym on the ground floor is open from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM on weekdays. Employees can book up to six free sessions per year with a counsellor through the Calmly app. Lunch is provided in the office cafeteria on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays.

Travel. Domestic business travel must be booked through the travel desk at least 7 days in advance. Employees travelling for work receive a daily allowance of 2,500 rupees for meals in India and 60 euros per day abroad. Flights shorter than 5 hours are booked in economy class. Train travel is preferred for trips between Pune and Mumbai.

Leaving the company. The notice period is 60 days for engineers and managers and 30 days for all other employees. Company laptops and access cards must be returned on or before the last working day. The final settlement, including any unused annual leave, is paid within 45 days of the last working day. Employees who leave can rejoin without a new probation period if they return within 12 months.

Contact. Questions about this handbook can be sent to the People Team at people@zorvex.example or raised in the #ask-people channel. This handbook was last updated in January 2026.
